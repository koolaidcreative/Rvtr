**Short bio (shown before "Read full bio")**
Geoffrey Thün is a designer, researcher and educator, and a director of RVTR, which he co-founded in 2007. He is Professor of Architecture at Taubman College of Architecture and Urban Planning and Associate Vice President for Research: Social Sciences, Humanities, and the Arts at the University of Michigan.

**Full bio (inside "Read full bio")**

His research applies complex-systems approaches across scales: regional territories and city planning, high-performance buildings, and full-scale prototypes of responsive, kinetic building envelopes. He co-leads RVTR’s projects, exhibitions, and publications, and shares in the recognition of the work. His academic research has attracted external funding from the U.S. Department of Energy’s National Renewable Energy Laboratory, National Science Foundation, U.S. Department of Transportation, U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, the National Research Council of Canada, the Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council of Canada, the Ontario Power Authority, Guardian Industries, Ford Motor Company, and the Department of Housing and Urban Development.

At Taubman College he was Associate Dean for Research and Creative Practice from 2014 to 2020 and Senior Associate Dean from 2020 to 2021. Prior to joining the University of Michigan in 2009, he taught at the University of Waterloo from 2006 to 2009, was Adjunct Associate Professor at Ryerson (now Toronto Metropolitan) University from 2011 to 2015, and has held visiting positions at California College of the Arts, Université du Québec à Montréal, Dalhousie University, Keio University and the International Solar University in Switzerland.

Before RVTR he worked at Dereck Revington Studio, where he led the Bloor Street Viaduct Safety Barriers project and was an associate at Baird Sampson Neuert Architects, where he was project lead for the French River Visitor Centre. He holds a B.A. in Sociology from the University of Western Ontario, a Bachelor of Environmental Studies and a professional Bachelor of Architecture from the University of Waterloo, and a Master of Urban Design from the University of Toronto.

**Role line**

**Short bio**
Kathy Velikov is an architect, designer, researcher and educator, and a director of RVTR, which she [fixed: doc says "he"] co-founded in 2007. She is Professor of Architecture and Associate Dean for Research and Creative Practice at Taubman College of Architecture and Urban Planning, University of Michigan, and directs the Institute for Advanced Construction Futures, a university-funded institute to advance innovation in the construction ecosystem. She is a licensed Architect in Michigan and Ontario, and was former President of ACADIA. In 2025 she was elevated to the College of Fellows of the American Institute of Architects.

**Full bio**

Kathy is a leader in practice-based design research and education, advancing environmentally responsive, technologically enabled, and climate positive built environments through speculation, prototyping, and visualization for responsive architectural material assemblies, resilient multifunctional urban infrastructures, and territorial practices for decarbonization. She co-leads RVTR’s projects, exhibitions, and publications, and shares in the recognition of the work. Her research has been funded by the National Fish and Wildlife Foundation, the City of Dearborn, Ford Motor Company, Guardian Industries, the American Institute of Architects, the Canadian Centre for Architecture, and the Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council of Canada.

She was elected to serve as President of ACADIA, the Association for Computer Aided Design in Architecture, from 2018 to 2020, and served as Vice President from 2021 to 2022. She has served as External Examiner for the MArch Architectural Design (B-Pro) program at The Bartlett, University College London, and Program Advisor to Florida Atlantic University’s School of Architecture. She has also held visiting faculty appointments at the California College of the Arts [fixed: doc says "Art"], Ryerson (now Toronto Metropolitan) University, and Adjunct appointments at the University of Waterloo and the University of Toronto.

Kathy joined the full time faculty at the University of Michigan’s Taubman College in 2009, and previously held the Oberdick Fellowship in 2006-2007. She was appointed Associate Dean for Research and Creative Practice in 2021.

Prior to co-founding RVTR, Kathy worked for over ten years in professional practice, working with ArchitectsAlliance, Sterling Finlayson [fixed: doc says "Finlayeson"] Architects, and Teeple Architects, where she built expertise in institutional buildings, housing, urban design, and planning. She holds a Bachelor of Environmental Studies and a professional Bachelor of Architecture from the University of Waterloo, and a Master of Art in the History of Art and Architecture from the University of Toronto.
