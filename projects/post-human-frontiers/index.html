Posthuman Frontiers: Data, Designers and Cognitive Machines

EXHIBITION DESIGN: ACADIA 2016 CONFERENCE

The ACADIA//2016 exhibition entitled “Posthuman Frontiers: Data, Designers and Cognitive Machines” curated by Sandra Manninger features design and research from the domains of practice and academia that are positioned at the intersection of procedural design, digital environments and autonomous machines. The exhibition includes both curated contributions and peer-reviewed projects submitted to the conference.
